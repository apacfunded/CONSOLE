                What happens next

                • Your wallet signs and pays about 0.03 SOL. Console never holds your funds.
                • Your coin goes live on pump.fun, with its website link pointing to its page on Console.
                • Its creator fees are split each time its pool fills: 10% buys back $CONSOLE, your chosen share funds AI work on the combo's games, and the rest is paid to your wallet. No finish date is promised.

                Risks and rules

                Console only works long term if it follows the same rules that keep fan decompilation projects alive.

US courts have treated taking software apart to understand it as fair use (Sega v. Accolade, 1992). This isn't legal advice.

                Where the money goes

                • Each coin's pump.fun creator fees go to a pool wallet Console controls for that coin.
                • When a pool reaches 0.5 SOL it is split: 10% buys back and burns $CONSOLE, the dev's chosen share (25 to 100% of the rest) goes to the AI compute wallet, and whatever is left is paid to the dev's own wallet.
                • The dev picks their AI share when launching. It is shown on the coin's card and can never be changed.
                • The AI compute wallet is controlled by the Console team and pays for the AI work. Buybacks and burns are done by hand and recorded on the Pools page, with a link to each transaction.
                • Console never takes a cut beyond the AI compute wallet. Every payout is public on Solscan.

                Memecoins are extremely risky

                Most coins lose most or all of their value, often within hours. A coin being on Console doesn't mean we vouch for it.

                No finished port is promised

                Fees fund the work. Big games can take a long time and a coin with little volume may stall. Progress is posted by the Console team from build results, not estimated.

                AI tasks every 1 SOL of volume

                Each coin earns one AI task for every 1 SOL traded, estimated from the creator fees collected. Tasks appear on the coin's AI work tab as queued, running or done, and say who did them. The AI works from public knowledge with no access to the games' files, so its notes are plans and research, not finished code.

                No game files, ever

                Console publishes code only. You need your own legally owned copy of a game (both games, for a combo) to build or play it.

                Approved games only

                No online, live-service or anti-cheat games. Coin names and art must be original, not a game's title or logo.

                Takedowns

                If a rights holder objects, that game's work and coin page are paused while we review it.

                Your wallet, your responsibility

                Console never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Connecting a wallet only shares your public address. We will never DM you first.
